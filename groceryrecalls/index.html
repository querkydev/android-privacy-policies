Privacy Policy

How GroceryRecalls handles your information

Effective: September 29, 2026
Important Notice: GroceryRecalls is an informational reference tool that surfaces publicly available USDA and FDA food safety notices. This app does not provide medical advice, safety guarantees, or definitive product safety certifications. All recall data is sourced from official government feeds and is provided for informational purposes only. Users should always verify product details against the official USDA or FDA notice.

1. Information We Collect

GroceryRecalls is designed to minimize data collection. Here is what the app stores and why:

Stored Locally on Your Device Only

✅ The following data is stored exclusively on your device and is never transmitted to any server:
  • Home State preference — the US state you select during onboarding, used to prioritize local recall notices. Your location is not automatically detected or transmitted.
  • Watched brands and ingredients — brands and ingredients you choose to monitor via the Watchdog feature.
  • Pantry items — products you manually add for recall cross-checking.
  • App preferences — theme, notification settings, and onboarding state.
  • Cached recall data — a local copy of publicly available USDA and FDA recall notices, refreshed periodically.

Push Notification Token

If you enable push notifications, the app registers a Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) device token with our servers. This is a randomly generated identifier used solely to deliver food safety alerts. It is not linked to any personal identity, name, email, or account.

Camera Access

The barcode scanning feature requests camera access. Camera data is processed entirely on-device to read barcodes. No images, photos, or video are stored or transmitted.

2. What We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, or any account credentials.
  • We do not collect, request, or transmit GPS coordinates or precise location data.
  • We do not use third-party advertising networks or behavioral tracking.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third party for marketing.
  • We do not store images or video from your camera.

3. Recall Data Sources

All food safety recall data is sourced from official US government APIs:

  • USDA Food Safety and Inspection Service (FSIS) — fsis.usda.gov
  • U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) — fda.gov

Recall data is fetched and cached periodically. There may be a delay between an official announcement and when it appears in this app. Always verify current recall status directly on USDA or FDA official websites.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) — delivers push notifications when enabled. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Cloudflare — hosts the app's backend API. Standard server access logs may be retained by Cloudflare per their data processing terms. GroceryRecalls does not access or store these logs.

5. Disclaimer of Liability

GroceryRecalls provides recall information for informational purposes only. We make no warranties regarding the completeness, accuracy, or timeliness of recall data.

The barcode scanner and product matching features check available cached federal recall notices. A result of "No Matching Recall Notices" does not constitute a safety guarantee or certification. Users must independently verify product details against official USDA and FDA notices.

Querky Dev's and GroceryRecalls shall not be liable for any injury, illness, loss, or damages arising from reliance on information provided by this application. Use of this app is at the user's own risk.

6. Push Notifications

If enabled, the app may send push notifications when new food safety recalls could match your watched brands, ingredients, or state. Notification delivery is subject to device settings, operating system behavior, battery management, and network availability. We do not guarantee delivery of any specific notification.

You can disable notifications at any time through your device's system settings.

7. Children's Privacy

GroceryRecalls does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. The app does not require account creation or submission of any personal data.
